Evaluation Query Set — Financial Document Intelligence Pipeline
Source documents: Apple Inc. 10-K filings, FY2024 (period ending 2024-09-28) and FY2025 (period ending 2025-09-27)
Format: one question per line. Each question has a verifiable answer in the source filings.

1. What was Apple's total net sales for fiscal year 2024?
2. What was Apple's total net sales for fiscal year 2025?
3. What was Apple's net income in fiscal year 2024?
4. What was Apple's diluted earnings per share for fiscal year 2025?
5. How much did Apple spend on research and development in fiscal year 2024?
6. What was Apple's iPhone segment net sales in fiscal year 2024?
7. What was Apple's Services segment net sales in fiscal year 2024?
8. What was Apple's gross margin percentage in fiscal year 2024?
9. What was Apple's total net sales for the Mac product line in fiscal year 2024?
10. What was Apple's total net sales for the Wearables, Home and Accessories category in fiscal year 2024?
11. How did Apple's total net sales change between fiscal year 2023 and fiscal year 2024?
12. What was Apple's total net sales for fiscal year 2023 (as reported in the FY2024 filing's comparative table)?
13. What operating expenses did Apple report in fiscal year 2024?
14. What was Apple's cash and cash equivalents position at the end of fiscal year 2024?
15. What risk factors did Apple disclose related to supply chain in the FY2024 filing?

Notes:
- Questions 1–10 have single, verifiable numeric answers directly extractable from the filings.
- Questions 11–12 test cross-reference / comparative retrieval across two years in the same table.
- Question 13–14 test retrieval from financial statement sections beyond the top-line revenue table.
- Question 15 tests retrieval from narrative (non-tabular) risk-factor text, as a contrast case to the numeric questions above.
- Only questions 1 and (partially) 6 were run to completion against both apple_10k_fixed and apple_semantic during this build; the remaining questions are provided as the intended full evaluation set for future completion (see Readme.md, Future Work).
